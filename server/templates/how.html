How it works

  • Daily limitSo much screen time a day, say an hour.
  • NightThe computer is usable from one time to another, say 6:00 to 21:00, and shuts down at the second.
  • CarryoverTime the child does not use goes to the next day, up to a cap you choose.
  • WeekdaysAny day can have its own limit or its own night, say a longer Saturday.
  • Extra timeWhen homework or a rainy day calls for more, you grant minutes from here, or take some away, and the computer picks it up within a minute if online.
